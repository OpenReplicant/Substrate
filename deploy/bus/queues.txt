# the `substrate` database (pgmq). Messages are JSON with at least "kind", "by" and "at". Adding a queue is a
# line here and `scripts/up.sh` (or `scripts/bus.sh`); removing one drops its messages, so do that by hand.

owner      what the owner sends from the console: proposed goals, notes for the next session
events     what happened in the stack, for the console's feed: verdicts, reviews, restarts
